# Response format and submission rules

Checked 8 November 2026: official [Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines) and [Dates and Deadlines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/Dates).

## Author response

Use OpenReview comments. The guide specifies a per-comment word limit without a numerical cap and permits multiple replies. No separate rebuttal PDF template is prescribed.

**Exact comment cap: unverified.** Check the live response form or author instructions before finalizing. Our overview plus reviewer-specific replies is a drafting choice; check Markdown rendering in OpenReview.

## Revised paper

Use the ICLR 2027 paper style; the revised main-text limit is **10 pages**, excluding references and appendices. Preserve anonymity. Multiple revisions are allowed during discussion; changes should be identified for reviewers and the AC. OpenReview generates a PDF comparison. Readers may disregard substantial departures or skip subsequent revisions. The guide requires an AI-use statement outside the page limit; consult its linked author AI policy when finalizing.

## Dates

Discussion runs **5–18 November 2026**. The dates page gives no precise cutoff time or timezone for this phase. Confirm those in OpenReview before planning a final upload.

## Local completion checks

Resolve placeholders, verify results against saved evidence, check comment limits and rendering, check the revised main-text page count, and attach manuscript locations to every claimed change. These are drafting notes; nothing has been posted or uploaded.
