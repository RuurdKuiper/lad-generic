# ICLR 2027 submission compliance check

Checked against the official ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines and AI Policy for Authors on 2026-09-18.

## Already satisfied in this draft

- Uses the official `iclr2027_conference` style copied from the supplied template.
- Anonymous author block; `\iclrfinalcopy` remains disabled.
- Self-citation is written in the third person.
- References are outside the main-text page allowance.
- Appendices follow the bibliography in the same PDF.
- The abstract is substantive rather than a placeholder.
- A required AI Use Statement and recommended Reproducibility and Ethics statements now appear immediately before the references.

## Must be checked immediately before submission

- Main text must be no more than 9 pages, excluding the references, AI statement, optional ethics statement, and reproducibility statement. Confirm from the final rendered PDF after all floats settle.
- Remove or resolve every red `XXX` marker.
- Keep the paper and all supplementary material anonymous, including URLs, repository metadata, acknowledgements, PDF metadata, and filenames exposed through links.
- Ensure every author has a current OpenReview profile. Authors cannot be added or removed after the abstract deadline; author order can change only until the full-paper deadline.
- Confirm reciprocal-reviewer registration and the conference's author/paper quotas in OpenReview.
- Upload source/code only in anonymized form, and keep text appendices after the bibliography.
- Repeat the AI-use disclosure consistently in the OpenReview submission form.
- Check dual-submission, code-of-ethics, licensing, and dataset-provenance obligations.
